Mooz brings smooth pinch-to-zoom to any mouse on Windows 10 and 11.

Download **Mooz-win-Setup.exe**, run it, then find Mooz in the system tray. Hold Shift and move your mouse up or down to zoom. Open Settings to choose another shortcut, change sensitivity, use the scroll wheel, or exclude applications.

This installer includes automatic update checks. When a new version is available, open Settings > Updates to download it and choose when to restart. Your preferences survive upgrades. Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. Mooz cannot send input to elevated apps unless it runs at the same privilege level. Compatibility with zoom gestures depends on the target application.
